## Editorial rule

1. Search for live footage from sources that explicitly allow reuse.
2. For every clip, retain source URL, license, source filename, and source in/out once downloaded.
3. Prefer real gardening footage over AI-generated visuals.
4. Use graphics only for:
   - soil-compaction mechanism,
   - simple measurement line,
   - small labels/check/X,
   - final branded CTA.
5. Do not use watercolor, sketchbook, NotebookLM-style slides, beige classroom cards, or generic AI-presenter visuals.
6. Do not imply that a person, brand, property, or product shown in stock footage endorses YardMasterPro.
7. If a stock clip only approximates a concept, label it as illustrative in production notes rather than presenting it as proof.

## Audio

Keep the approved English narration from the existing Short unless a timing mismatch forces a minor edit.

Approved narration:

> Think your raised bed is set up right? These seven mistakes can damage your soil before next season.  
> Number one: using old railroad ties or CCA-treated lumber around your food garden.  
> Number two: making the bed so wide that you have to step inside it. Foot traffic compacts the soil and reduces the pore space your plants depend on.  
> Number three: filling the bed with heavy native clay instead of a balanced growing mix.  
> Number four: mixing wood chips directly into the active growing soil.  
> Number five: automatically adding heavy amounts of compost every year without checking what your soil needs.  
> Number six: watering on a fixed schedule instead of responding to actual soil moisture.  
> And number seven: leaving raised-bed soil completely bare through winter.  
> Want to know exactly how to fix all seven? Watch the full guide on YardMasterPro.

## Remotion assembly rules

- Composition: `1080x1920`, `30fps`, target `~60 sec`.
- Maintain the existing narration timing as the master timeline.
- Replace source scenes with local files from `public/broll/`.
- Use `objectFit: cover`; crop for vertical without cutting off hands, tools, or the subject.
- Avoid reusing the same exact source moment more than once unless explicitly noted.
- No stock-platform watermarks or UI.
- Keep primary captions inside Shorts safe area.
- Primary text: 2–5 words whenever possible.
- Prefer hard cuts, subtle push-ins, and simple match cuts; no flashy template transitions.
- Keep original clip audio muted unless intentionally used as very low ambience beneath narration.
- Music, if used, must be independently licensed and mixed well below narration.
- Final CTA remains branded but visually restrained.
